Medicare claims target trial emulation

ACE inhibitor vs thiazide initiation on CMS synthetic Medicare data: new-user active-comparator cohort, IPTW/IPCW, weighted risk curves, bootstrap, sensitivity analyses, with independent re-derivation in Python and SAS.

Synthetic data. Nothing here is a real-world finding. The estimates demonstrate methods; CMS advises against scientific inference from the Synthea-generated files.
